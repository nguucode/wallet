What we heard

The brief

A demo e-wallet for young urban professionals in Vietnam. The app's promise is that every state is clear: no hidden pending, every transaction carries one status line. The icon has to sit on an iPhone home screen next to MoMo and bank apps and still feel calm and native.

clearcalmnativetrustworthy

Success: reads as an iOS icon at a glance, ownable in the violet Violet Sky world, recognisable at 16 px, survives in one colour.

Concept A

Folded W

A W folded from a single ribbon: one continuous path, each fold visible, nothing hidden.

  • One ribbon, every fold shown: the 'every state is clear' promise as form
  • Two tones give the iOS-native depth without effects; the fold survives in one colour as a cut
  • Four identical panels on one angle system, so it grows into patterns, loaders and motion (the ribbon folding in)

Recommendation & next steps

Our recommendation

Folded W, approved direction. Masters, lockups and the web/iOS icon set are delivered; the app already uses the new icon.

  • Review the icon on a real home screen (add to Home Screen on iPhone)
  • Trademark and reverse-image search before any public use
  • Optional: fold-in motion for the splash and the loading state
